## Introduction

I am a student at Solent University studying Computer Science at the undergraduate level. I am passionate about accessibility, user-centric design, and privacy/security. One of my goals is to build a home system that provides privacy and security on the internet, as well as a cloud solution that removes some reliance on third-party software such as subscription services and streaming platforms.
